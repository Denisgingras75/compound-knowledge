# Constitution

## Amendment clause

Only Denis edits this section. The curator and the review skill follow it.

1. An article needs evidence from a real session: a correction, a mistake the model made, or a reverted change. A session's opinion alone is not evidence.
2. Only record what the model gets wrong in Denis's work: his conventions, stack, preferences and past mistakes. Skip general knowledge the model already follows.
3. Write each article as one line, `When X → do Y — because Z`. Keep the reason; it is what lets the rule generalize.
4. Nothing becomes an article until Denis approves it in a review.
5. Articles never expire for going quiet. Repeal one only when the code or tool it names is gone, when the monthly check shows it changed nothing, or when Denis says so.
6. An article that keeps being broken while loaded goes back to Denis for rewording or repeal. It is not strengthened.
7. When two articles conflict, keep both and flag them for Denis.
